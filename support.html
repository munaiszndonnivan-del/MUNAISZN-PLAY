Common Problems

1. Search is not working

  • Check that your device has an active internet connection.
  • Open Settings and check your TMDB API key.
  • Make sure the key is valid and has not been disabled.
  • Refresh the website and try again.

2. Posters or movie information are missing

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Try searching for the title again.
  • Some catalogue information may temporarily be unavailable from third-party providers.

3. A video player is not working

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Try another browser if the player does not load.
  • Some external players may be unavailable or unsupported.
  • Only access video sources you are authorized to use.

4. My List or viewing progress disappeared

Saved lists and viewing progress may be stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing browser data, changing browsers or switching devices may remove or hide locally saved information.

5. Installation or offline problems

  • Use an up-to-date browser.
  • Check that the website has finished loading at least once while connected to the internet.
  • Some features, including catalogue searches and external video services, still require internet access.

Before Reporting a Problem

Please include:

  • A short description of what went wrong.
  • The page or feature where the issue occurred.
  • Your device and browser type.
  • The exact error message, if one appeared.
  • Steps to reproduce the problem.

Never include passwords, private API keys or other confidential information in a public issue report.

About the Service

MUNAISZN PLAY is a movie and television discovery interface. Catalogue information and external services may be provided by independent third parties. Their availability is not guaranteed.
