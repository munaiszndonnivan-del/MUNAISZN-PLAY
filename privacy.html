Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MUNAISZN PLAY handles information when you visit and use our movie and television discovery website.

MUNAISZN PLAY currently operates as a browser-based discovery experience. It does not currently provide its own registered user accounts or cloud synchronisation.

1. Information stored on your device

MUNAISZN PLAY uses your browser's local storage to remember certain preferences and features on the device you use. These may include:

  • Your TMDB API key, if you choose to enter one in Settings.
  • Your personal watchlist.
  • Your recently opened titles and saved episode progress.

This information is stored locally in your browser. Clearing browser data or changing devices may remove or make this information unavailable. It is not currently synchronised to a MUNAISZN PLAY cloud account.

2. Third-party services

MUNAISZN PLAY uses The Movie Database (TMDB) to retrieve movie, television, artwork, ratings and related catalogue information. When you connect your TMDB API key, requests are made to TMDB's services.

TMDB operates under its own privacy policy and terms. Please review them at TMDB Privacy Policy.

Some features may connect to external services, including YouTube for trailers and external video players when selected. Those services may receive information such as your IP address and browser information when you access their content. Their own privacy policies and practices apply.

3. Cookies and browser storage

The current website uses browser local storage for its saved preferences and viewing information. Third-party services may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you access them.

4. How information is used

Locally stored information helps the website remember your watchlist, recently opened titles and catalogue connection settings. Third-party requests are used to retrieve the content or services you choose to access.

5. Data sharing

MUNAISZN PLAY does not currently operate a cloud account system or sell user information. Information sent to third-party services is subject to those services' own practices. We cannot control how independent third-party providers handle information.

6. Children's privacy

MUNAISZN PLAY is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of third-party content and services.

7. Your choices

  • You can remove your saved watchlist and browser data through your browser settings.
  • You can clear your saved TMDB API key through the website's Settings.
  • You can choose whether to use external trailers or video players.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as MUNAISZN PLAY introduces new features, integrations or services. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact and support

For privacy questions or support requests, contact the project through the MUNAISZN PLAY GitHub support page.
